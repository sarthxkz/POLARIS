# Data files in /public/data

Built by `scripts/build-natural-earth.mjs` from Natural Earth (https://www.naturalearthdata.com/),
which is in the public domain. Source files: `ne_50m_admin_0_boundary_lines_land`,
`ne_110m_admin_0_countries`, `ne_110m_populated_places_simple` (GeoJSON from
github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector, fetched 2026-10-24).

Boundaries follow Natural Earth's de facto conventions; disputed areas are drawn as Natural Earth draws them.
